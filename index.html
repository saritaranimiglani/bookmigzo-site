Migzo

Order ahead, skip the queue.

Migzo was an order-ahead, self-pickup food app in India. Customers picked a nearby restaurant, paid in the app, and collected their food at the counter without waiting. Restaurants ran their orders from a web dashboard.

Migzo closed on 20 November 2026. The app is kept on the app stores as a showcase of the work. It runs entirely offline with sample restaurants and menus. Sign-in, ordering and payments are turned off, and nothing you do in it is sent to us.
